# Issue Template

## Title
Short, specific, and technical.

## Root Cause
What actually caused the problem.

## Engineering Impact
How it affects velocity, reliability, cost, or scalability.

## Reproduction
Minimal scenario that demonstrates the issue.

## Observed Behavior
What happens today.

## Expected Behavior
What should happen instead.

## Severity
Excellent / Good / Risky / Dangerous / Critical

## Suggested Fixes
- immediate mitigation,
- structural fix,
- long-term prevention.

## Tests Required
- unit,
- integration,
- invariant,
- regression.

## Owner
Who is responsible for the fix and validation.
