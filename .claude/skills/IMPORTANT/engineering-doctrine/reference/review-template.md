# Review Template

## 1. Product
- What problem is being solved?
- Why now?
- What does success mean?

## 2. Architecture
- Why this design?
- What was rejected?
- What are the scaling limits?

## 3. Modularity
- Are boundaries clean?
- Are responsibilities isolated?
- Are interfaces minimal?

## 4. Complexity
- What is hard to understand?
- What is hidden?
- What is coupled by timing or state?

## 5. Failure Modes
- What breaks first?
- What cascades?
- What is the rollback path?

## 6. Decision
- Approve
- Approve with conditions
- Block
