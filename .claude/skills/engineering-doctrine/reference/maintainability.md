# Maintainability

## Questions
- How hard is onboarding?
- How hard is debugging?
- What will developers fear touching?
- What will rot first?
- What requires tribal knowledge?
- What is undocumented?

## Signals of Low Maintainability
- large files,
- unclear naming,
- hidden side effects,
- inconsistent patterns,
- poor testability,
- too many special cases,
- unclear ownership.

## Rule
A maintainable system is one where a new engineer can safely change behavior without reverse-engineering the entire codebase.
